Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how PixOLine handles information when you use the PixOLine graphic design application and visit the PixOLine website.

Important: PixOLine is designed as a free graphic design application for Android phones and tablets. PixOLine does not require an account or registration to get started.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PixOLine. PixOLine is a graphic design application designed for Android phones and tablets. It provides tools for creating and editing illustrations, graphics, posters, banners, social media designs and other creative projects.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use PixOLine, visit our website, or interact with services associated with PixOLine.

By using PixOLine, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

PixOLine is designed so that you can start creating without creating an account or registering with us.

Depending on how you use the application and related services, information may be processed by the device, operating system, or third-party services required to provide specific functions.

We do not ask you to provide personal information simply to use the core graphic design features of PixOLine.

3. Account Information

PixOLine does not require users to create an account in order to use the application.

You can install PixOLine and begin creating designs without providing a username, password, or profile information to PixOLine.

4. Your Designs and Files

PixOLine allows you to create and edit your own designs, including graphics, illustrations, images, text, shapes and other creative content.

Your projects may contain information that you personally add to them. You are responsible for the content you create, import, save, export, or share using PixOLine.

PixOLine supports working with images, SVG graphics, templates, icons, fonts and other design elements. When you import content into PixOLine, you should make sure that you have the necessary rights or permission to use that content.

5. Device Permissions

Certain PixOLine features may require access to device resources in order to perform the requested function.

For example, access may be required when you choose to import images, save or export your work, or use a feature that requires interaction with files stored on your device.

Permissions are intended to support the functionality requested by the user. You can manage application permissions through the Android operating system settings on your device.

6. Photos, Images and Other User Content

PixOLine provides tools for importing and editing images and incorporating them into your designs.

You remain responsible for the images, graphics, text, fonts and other content that you choose to import or use.

Do not use PixOLine to store, process, or distribute content that you do not have the legal right to use.

7. Locally Stored Projects

PixOLine supports standalone project files that can be kept for future editing.

When a project is saved to your device, the project is stored according to the storage mechanisms and permissions provided by the Android operating system.

You should regularly back up important project files. PixOLine is not responsible for loss of locally stored files resulting from device failure, accidental deletion, operating-system changes, or other circumstances outside our control.

8. Exporting and Sharing

PixOLine allows you to export and share finished designs in supported formats such as PDF, PNG and JPG.

When you choose to share or export content, the destination and handling of that content may be controlled by the application, operating system, or third-party application that you select.

We recommend reviewing the privacy practices of third-party services before sharing personal or confidential information.

9. Third-Party Services

PixOLine may rely on third-party services for certain functions, distribution, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or other technical services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Where applicable, you should review the privacy policies of those services to understand how they handle information.

10. Google Play

PixOLine may be distributed through Google Play. Google and the Android platform may process information in connection with downloading, installing, updating, and using applications.

Such processing is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies rather than this Privacy Policy.

11. Website Information

When you visit the PixOLine website, your web browser and hosting infrastructure may automatically process standard technical information needed to deliver web pages and maintain website security.

This may include information such as browser type, device type, operating system, IP address, requested pages, and basic technical information associated with a web request.

Such information may be processed by the website hosting provider or other infrastructure providers according to their applicable policies.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The PixOLine website may use cookies or similar technologies when required for website functionality, security, analytics, or other legitimate website operations.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.

13. Children's Privacy

PixOLine is a general-purpose graphic design application and is not intended to require children to submit personal information to us.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating PixOLine accounts because PixOLine does not require an account to use its core functionality.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that requires our attention, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

14. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information associated with PixOLine services.

However, no electronic storage, transmission, application, or internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should take appropriate precautions when storing or sharing sensitive information.

15. Data Retention

Because PixOLine does not require an account for its core functionality, there is generally no PixOLine account profile that you need to maintain.

Information processed by third-party services, website hosting providers, app distribution platforms, or other service providers may be retained according to their respective policies and legal requirements.

16. Your Choices

You have control over how you use PixOLine and what content you create, import, save, export, and share.

  • You can choose whether to grant optional device permissions.
  • You can choose which images and files to import.
  • You can choose where to save or export your designs.
  • You can choose whether to share your finished designs.
  • You can manage application permissions through Android settings.
  • You can manage browser cookies through your browser settings.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to PixOLine, its website, applicable services, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective or update date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically so that you remain informed about how PixOLine handles information.

18. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or PixOLine's privacy practices, please contact the PixOLine team through the contact information provided on our website.

You can visit our Contact Us page for available contact options.

19. Acceptance of This Policy

By using PixOLine or visiting the PixOLine website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of PixOLine and its associated services.
