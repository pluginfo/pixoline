PixOLine Support

Need help with PixOLine? Find answers to common questions, learn how to use the app, and get assistance with your projects and designs.

Welcome to PixOLine Support. PixOLine is designed to make graphic design simple and accessible on Android phones and tablets. Browse the information below for help with common questions and issues.

1. Getting Started

PixOLine allows you to create graphics, illustrations, posters, banners, social media designs, and other creative projects directly from your Android device.

To get started, install PixOLine and create a new project. You can then add text, images, shapes, icons, colors, and other design elements to your canvas.

🖌 Create a Design

Start a new project and use the available design tools to create your artwork.

📁 Open a Project

Open a previously saved PixOLine project and continue editing your design.

🖼 Import Images

Import supported images from your device and use them within your creative projects.

📤 Export Your Work

Export finished designs into supported formats for saving or sharing.

2. Saving Projects

PixOLine supports saving your projects so you can continue working on them later.

Projects saved on your device are subject to the storage permissions and file management provided by the Android operating system.

We recommend keeping backup copies of important projects, especially before making significant changes to your device or operating system.

3. Importing Images

You can import images from your device when creating or editing a design.

  • Make sure PixOLine has the required file or media permission.
  • Use supported image formats whenever possible.
  • Check that the image still exists on your device.
  • For large images, allow additional time for processing.

4. Exporting Designs

When your design is complete, you can export it using one of the supported output formats available in PixOLine.

Depending on the version of the application and project type, available export options may include formats such as PNG, JPG, or PDF.

Tip: If an exported image does not appear immediately, check your device's Downloads, Pictures, Documents, or the folder selected during the export process.

5. Permissions

Some PixOLine features may require access to files, photos, media, or other device resources.

If a feature does not work as expected, check the PixOLine permissions in your Android device settings and make sure the required permission has been granted.

You can change application permissions at any time through Android settings.

6. Common Issues

PixOLine is running slowly

Try closing other applications, restarting your device, and reopening PixOLine. Very large projects or images may require additional device resources.

My project is not opening

Make sure the project file has not been moved, renamed, deleted, or damaged. If you have a backup copy, try opening the backup project.

Images are not importing

Check the application's permissions and verify that the image file is accessible from your device. You can also try importing another image to determine whether the problem is specific to one file.

Export is not working

Make sure your device has sufficient available storage and that PixOLine has the required permissions. Try exporting a smaller or simpler project if the issue continues.

7. Updating PixOLine

Keeping PixOLine updated can provide bug fixes, performance improvements, new features, and compatibility improvements.

If you installed PixOLine through Google Play, check the application's Google Play page for available updates.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use PixOLine?

No. PixOLine is designed so that you can use its core graphic design features without creating an account.

Can I use PixOLine offline?

Many core design functions are intended to work locally on your device. Certain features or services may require an internet connection.

Where are my projects stored?

Projects that you save locally are stored according to the location and storage options selected through PixOLine and the Android operating system.

Can I share my designs?

Yes. You can export your designs and use the sharing options provided by your device and supported applications.

9. Still Need Help?

If you cannot find an answer to your question, please contact the PixOLine team with as much useful information as possible.

When contacting support, it can be helpful to include your PixOLine version, Android version, device model, and a short description of the problem.

Visit our Contact Us page for available contact options.

10. Support Information

PixOLine support information may be updated as new application versions and features become available. Please check this page periodically for updated troubleshooting information.

We appreciate your feedback and suggestions as we continue to improve PixOLine.
